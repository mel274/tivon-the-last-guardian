# Location and historical reference register

Research date: 24 October 2026. This register describes evidence, not survey-grade geometry. The current game is an interpretive prototype. Its building models, elevations, interiors, paths and distances must not be advertised as accurate reconstructions.

## Classification

- **Verified**: the specific fact is supported by the linked source. Verification of a business identity does not verify its exact building footprint or present frontage.
- **Approximate**: original scenery or spatial arrangement used to make the prototype playable; not a surveyed representation.
- **Fictional**: original characters, dialogue, plot, mechanisms, underground chambers and quest objects.

## Amali / עמלי

**Identity:** Confirmed against the user's link as **עמלי בית קפה - Amali Roasters**, a family specialty coffee café and roaster in Kiryat Tivon. Its own website and the local council tourism directory agree on the name and business character. Do not replace this with a similarly named café.

**Address evidence:** The business's own [About page](https://www.amaliroast.com/about-us), [Terms page](https://www.amaliroast.com/terms-conditions) and [municipal tourism entry](https://tour.ktv.org.il/business/56/) give **כיכר בן גוריון 1, קרית טבעון — 1 Ben Gurion Square, Kiryat Tivon**. A [third-party Easy listing](https://easy.co.il/page/10153126) instead labels it **הרקפות 2 — 2 HaRakafot**. This discrepancy has not been resolved; it may be addressing of the same commercial vicinity, but that is only a possibility, not a verified fact.

**User reference:** [Supplied Google share link](https://share.google/x8sky23zZIwOLDvFS). The research browser initially failed to fetch this URL. A subsequently approved direct HTTP request resolved it through Google's share endpoint to a search page explicitly carrying the query **עמלי בית קפה - Amali Roasters**. This confirms the intended business identity. The request did not expose a surveyed coordinate or frontage image, so exact placement and architecture are still unverified.

**Visual evidence available:** The business's About page includes image descriptions for ceramic cups, wooden shelving, a potted plant, café window seating and espresso preparation. It describes an in-house small kitchen and coffee roasting. The images themselves could not be fetched in this research session, so these descriptions are only limited reference evidence. Exterior dimensions, color scheme, entrance configuration, awning and surrounding shop arrangement remain unverified.

**Confidence:** High for business identity, including the supplied link, and its self-published address; low for physical façade and footprint. Address-label discrepancy remains unresolved.

**Additional evidence (0.2):** A [Maariv article](https://www.maariv.co.il/food/foodnews/article-1232411) places the café at 1 Ben Gurion Square in the old centre and says the space was previously a cinema and then a second-hand shop; it mentions two grinders and a seasonal pastry display. Amali's own site mentions a Stronghold S8X roaster and shows two-tone ceramic cups and light wood. The project owner supplied the café's logo (white Hebrew wordmark on blue, with Arabic and English) and asked for the brand's purple; both are used on the storefront. Google places the business at about 32.72299 N, 35.13091 E.

**Modeling status:** Suitable for a clearly labeled, replaceable Amali gameplay prototype with original interior geometry. Version 0.2 models a tall single hall (a nod to the former cinema) with a black-steel glazed front, the supplied logo, purple accents, an oak counter, espresso machine, two grinders, pastry case, a compact drum roaster and a shelf wall of bags and cups. Counter, espresso equipment, grinder, beans and seating are gameplay approximations. Do not claim a faithful exterior reconstruction. All staff dialogue and NPCs are fictional; no real employee is portrayed.

## Mandu Bar / מנדו בר

**Verified address:** **אלכסנדר זייד 1, קרית טבעון — 1 Alexander Zaid Street, Kiryat Tivon**, from the [municipal tourism directory](https://tour.ktv.org.il/business/45/).

**Verified character:** The directory describes a neighborhood pub hosting music, drinks, exhibitions and events; an upstairs vintage/secondhand clothing shop; and a daytime café, Café Beinenu. The council's [coffee directory](https://tour.ktv.org.il/business/category/7/) describes that café in a wooden cabin within a garden at the rear of Mandu.

**Reference leads:** The council entry links the business's [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mandu_bar/) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/people/%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%95-%D7%91%D7%A8-%D7%98%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9F/61574181124482/). Images on these accounts were not verified here. A third-party hosted promotional site appears in search, but its ownership was not established and it was not used to establish façade details or current events.

**Confidence:** High for the address and mixed pub/café/vintage character; incomplete for exact current frontage, dimensions and floor plan.

**Modeling status (0.2 → Mandu Interior Plan rev 3):** At the project owner's direction the bar is on the upper floor of the 1 Alexander Zaid building, reached by an outside stair and terrace above the ground-floor shops (Butcher Tivon, which OSM also lists at Alexander Zaid 1, and others). The interior follows the owner-approved [Mandu Interior Plan](mandu-plan/mandu-plan.html) (revision 3) and the owner's answers:

- Narrow north–south bar strip (5.4 × 12.36 m) between the printing shop and Itzik's camera shop; door at the west end of the glass front; hanging wooden sign edge-on at the west end.
- Dark-green west wall with a steep stair to a standable loft (~2.3 m up; strip ceiling raised to ~4.6 m); clothes hangers and tables on the loft; chandelier.
- Bar counter under the loft with stools and taps; snack counter = the back bar on the west wall (worktop + one bottle shelf).
- Toilet 1.5 × 1.4 m in the north-west corner; short corridor south of it to the backyard door, with a sink at the west end and the fridge at the east end beside that door.
- White east wall: kegs, a standing wine cooler, and two gooseneck lamps. Only those two cold units (wine cooler + back-door fridge).
- Flipped-L bench along the east wall then the front glass, with three small tables.
- Backyard: Café Beinenu is a wooden coffee cart (~2.6 × 2.8 m) with its service window and ledge on the east side; customers queue in the backyard and use the yard tables (the cart has no seating of its own). Day/night signs still swap at dusk.
- Storyteller stays on the balcony at his existing seat so the Mandu story keeps working.

Built this way in 0.4.0. The plot-cost and terrain-mesh tasks did not change the interior.

A 15–30-person crowd is a game design choice, not a factual crowd count. Fictional drinking, conversations and mysteries are not claims about the business.

## Beit She'arim National Park / גן לאומי בית שערים

**Verified navigation coordinate:** Israel Nature and Parks Authority publishes **32.7027036, 35.1275862** as a Waze navigation destination. This is a visitor navigation point, not a surveyed cave threshold or every chamber's coordinate. [Official park page](https://www.parks.org.il/reserve-park/%D7%92%D7%9F-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/).

**Verified historical and visual foundation:** The [UNESCO property page](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1471/) describes catacombs cut into limestone hills at the edge of the Jezreel Valley, developed from the second century CE. It identifies inscriptions in Greek, Aramaic, Hebrew and Palmyrene, and the site's connection to Rabbi Judah the Patriarch. The park page identifies the Cave of Coffins, Menorah Caves and burial chambers, as well as the synagogue, basilica and olive press.

**Terrain reference:** An [Israel Antiquities Authority excavation report](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=26041) describes Sheikh Abreik hill and easily quarried chalk and nari bedrock on the western edge of the valley. The report is useful for materials and topographic relationships; it is not a license to reproduce its figures.

**Confidence:** High for the site's broad history, material palette and regional setting. No room-by-room measured model or GIS/terrain import has been established in this prototype.

**Modeling status:** Original limestone/chalk-inspired cave geometry and sarcophagi are approximations. Puzzle inscriptions, switches and lock mechanisms must be identified as fictional. The secret cave network, supernatural events and treasure are entirely fictional. Avoid turning actual tomb contents into loot or implying that the game reveals real archaeological discoveries. The prototype's night visit does not describe real opening hours or permission to enter.

## Alexander Zeid statue and historical background

**Verified setting:** The [Israeli Ministry of Defense Izkor memorial page](https://www.izkor.gov.il/en_921b5f77b47ff5f91eb85c2711506143) locates the memorial on the Sheikh Abreik/Zeid hill near the entrance to Beit She'arim, north of Beit Zaid, south of Kiryat Tivon and Route 75, and west of Route 722. No exact statue coordinate is invented here.

**Verified appearance:** The same source describes a bronze rider and horse atop a large stone base on an elevated, fenced hilltop area. The rider looks east; the base has reliefs connected with farming and shepherding. These are appropriate cues for an original simplified statue model. No copyrighted photographs are included in the game by this research task.

**Verified biography for journal use:** Izkor records Zeid's arrival in 1904, involvement in Bar Giora and Hashomer, establishment of a farm in this area in 1926, and his work guarding the area for the Jewish National Fund. It records his death in 1938. The [official INPA statue interpretation](https://www.parks.org.il/article/bet-sheaarim-audio-5/) provides another interpretive reference. The [Hebrew University Center for Jewish Art record](https://cja.huji.ac.il/sch/browser.php?id=56980&mode=set) is an additional monument reference.

**Nearby relationship:** [KKL-JNF's Landau Forest guide](https://kkl-jnf.org/tourism-and-recreation/forests-and-parks/landau-forest/) discusses the HaShomrim/Yizrael junction memorial and the nearby watchmen's cemetery. This memorial is a distinct location from the equestrian statue; do not merge them.

**Confidence:** High for those broad biographical facts, statue type and regional location. Any detailed biography or specific artifact should be separately checked before inclusion.

**Modeling/narrative status:** The ghost, cursed duty, invented letter, lantern, memory objects and peaceful supernatural ending are fictional interpretations. Zeid is treated as a tragic protective figure. A late-story identity reveal can coexist with a historically labeled statue: avoid identifying the monster with the statue before the designed reveal.

## Version 0.3 survey notes

- **Scale:** 1:1. Origin: Beit She'arim catacombs (32.70340 N, 35.12878 E) at game point (126, 86). OSM building footprints and paths are used as mapped. Elevation is the GEM-15 bare-earth DTM (15 m source, resampled), with each road smoothed along its length and each private house on a flat terrace. Small walls and garden edges are still authored by hand. The earlier AWS Terrain Tiles DEM is kept only so the build can be repeated; the game does not sample it.
- **Ben Gurion Square:** the pedestrian street runs NW–SE between HaShkedim and Get Phone (32.72271 N, 35.13014 E), positioned from Google Street View (7 HaShkedim St) and the satellite view; shop placement follows Google Maps pins. OSM maps this block as one merged building, which the game replaces.
- **Zeid centre (1 Alexander Zaid):** built to the owner-approved Zeid Centre Plan, revision 4.2 (`docs/zeid-plan/zeid-plan.html`; pins, answers and photos are in the plan artifact's data). The layout is encoded in `ZEID` in `src/world/layout.ts`:
  - Route 75 bus stop → short stair → upper-plaza walkway.
  - Upper floor, west to east: Butcher Tivon, printing, Mandu (narrow north–south bar strip, Café Beinenu by day), Itzik's camera shop, Pilates, building supplies, all opening onto a full-length balcony entered from the upper plaza.
  - Backyard behind the building, level with the plaza, below a bank of bushes and the road railing; side entrance at the west end.
  - Stair outside the balcony, west to east, to the lower plaza.
  - Ground floor, west to east: a closed section (Rav-Kav centre, flowers) with an east-facing door in the roofed strip under the balcony; then Zeid 1, Mishi and Oranim pharmacy, as deep as the upper floor.
  - Drugstore (candy, Toto, ATM) north of the lane, falafel row south of it; Zohar cinema faces the building.
- **Private houses:** agreed 2026-10-25 and built in 0.4.0. A footprint is a private house when it is about 40–250 m², at most three storeys, and not tagged as a shop, a public building or apartments. Each gets a garden plot (street wall, hedge or fence from the street survey, a gate and path, lawn and garden trees) and a flat terrace with rubble retaining walls on the edges that step. The 2026-10-26 count is 1,835 houses and 1,832 plots. Where a stepped wall would run into a road or a neighbour, that edge stays one wall; the tallest measured is about 15.7 m.
- **Zeid centre, owner's photos (2026-10-25)**, modelled with the 0.4.0 centre (beige balcony tiles, canvas awnings, roller shutters, a landing on the lower-plaza stair, the longer bus-stop stair with green railings). What the photos show:
  - *Balcony:* beige floor tiles; white ceiling with red-painted beams and fluorescent tubes; square piers of stacked dark wooden slats; a stucco parapet about 1 m high with a wide wooden plank cap (not a metal railing); rolled cream canvas awnings between the piers; string lights; wall fans and roller shutters on the shopfronts; stacked folding chairs. A brown hanging sign "קפה בינינו · מנדו בר · אמנות וינטג׳" beside the door. A yellow "טמבור" sign further along.
  - *Stair to the lower plaza:* terrazzo steps between stucco walls with stainless handrails, with a wide landing at the top.
  - *Lower plaza:* red-brown pavers with light stripes forming large diamonds, mature trees with seating rings, the cinema's low pergola front.
  - *Upper floor seen from the upper plaza:* a one-storey façade of wooden-slat piers, a slatted fascia band and roller shutters. Upper plaza paving is red brick in front of the shops and grey elsewhere; a clipped hedge lines the stair down.
  - *By the bus stop:* a long stair with green railings climbs from the upper plaza to Route 75. Beside it is a small kiosk with yellow stone-pattern walls, a clock and a red-tile hipped roof, with a yellow mailbox, rubble boulders and an oleander.
  - *Falafel row:* a single-storey row under a flat canopy, facing north onto the lane, with picnic tables. Revision 4.2 settled the order. West to east from Alexander Zaid Street: Falafel Chen (its signboard faces the street and the lane), AURUM clocks, מספרת סלים, Falafel Luli (blue scalloped awning), a unit for rent, Beit Chabad, then the pizza unit sticking out at the east end with its window facing back along the lane. The drugstore (הצומת) is north of the lane, with its porch on the lane.

## World scale and approximation ledger

- The prototype uses a coherent local game coordinate system, not geographic coordinates. World coordinates are not latitude/longitude.
- Landmark spacing, slopes, building footprints and pedestrian routes are compressed/original until measured geographic data is added. There is currently no verified compression ratio.
- Preserve the supported broad relationship: the town lies north of the Beit She'arim/Zeid hill area, which overlooks the Jezreel Valley. Do not claim exact street turns from the prototype map.
- Israeli residential forms, stone retaining walls, planted Mediterranean vegetation, Hebrew signs and sloped roads are regional visual cues, not evidence for a specific frontage.
- Version 0.2 imports OpenStreetMap street centrelines (see ASSET_SOURCES.md for provenance and the ODbL credit). Transformation: equirectangular projection about 32.7027 N, 35.1276 E (anchored to the Beit She'arim catacombs at game point 126, 80), uniform ×0.3 compression, then radial magnification lenses (×2.2 inside, blending to identity) around Ben Gurion Square and Kiryat Amal. Topology and relative placement follow OSM; distances inside the lenses and the ring around them are not to scale. No elevation raster is used; relief is interpretive.
